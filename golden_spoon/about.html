Our Story

A devotion to flavour, hospitality and beautiful moments.

A shared table

Born from a love of gathering.

What began as a 22-seat room in Bandra has grown into a destination for diners who believe a meal should be felt as much as tasted.

At The Royal Spoon, Indian warmth meets contemporary culinary craft. We honour familiar flavours while giving them new light, bringing together the comfort of tradition and the thrill of discovery.
